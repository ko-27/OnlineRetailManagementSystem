\# Inventory Management



\## Purpose

Manages the availability and stock information of products.



\## Responsibilities

\- Track product stock

\- Update inventory levels

\- Identify low-stock products

## Inventory Monitoring Workflow



1\. Product stock is recorded.

2\. Stock levels are updated after order processing.

3\. Low-stock products are identified.

4\. Inventory information is kept available for product management.
